About

Minh Tam

Urban researcher and GIS specialist based in Biên Hòa, Việt Nam. Working at the intersection of spatial data, city systems, and design.

Introduction

I am Minh Tam (also known as Alice), an urban studies researcher based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. My research focuses on public transport, urban networks, and policy-making, including urban planning, human mobility, governance, population, and land law.

I build tools and conduct research that makes complex urban dynamics legible — from flood risk modeling in the Mekong Delta to transit network analysis in rapidly growing Vietnamese cities.
